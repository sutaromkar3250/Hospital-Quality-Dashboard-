

Project Title 
Hospital Quality Data Analysis

Short Description
The project focuses on analyzing hospital operational performance, patient outcomes, healthcare quality indicators, and patient satisfaction. MySQL is used for database management, data cleaning, transformation, and analytical queries, while Power BI is used to create interactive dashboards and visual reports.

Technology
MySQL, Power BI

Features
Monitor hospital quality and patient safety.
Analyze patient admission and discharge trends.
Track readmission and mortality rates.
Measure patient satisfaction.
Analyze hospital department performance.
Identify areas requiring quality improvement.
Support data-driven hospital management decisions.
 
Business Problem
How many patients were admitted each month?
Which department has the highest admission volume?
What is the hospital's 30-day readmission rate?
What is the average length of stay?
Which departments have higher infection rates?
What is the hospital mortality rate?
What is the average patient satisfaction score?
How does hospital performance change over time?
What are the bed occupancy trends?
Which quality indicators require management attention?

Dashboard Goal
Identify departments with higher admission volumes.
Understand monthly hospital admission patterns.
Monitor patient safety and quality indicators.
Compare department-wise operational performance.
Identify opportunities to improve patient satisfaction.	
Support evidence-based hospital management decisions.
 
Business Impact
Improved Operational efficiency: identify department with higher admission volumes ,monitor average patient stay duration ,support department level workload planning ,identify opportunities to improve patient flow 
Improved patient safety monitoring: identify department with higher safety incident rate ,support quality improvement investigation 
Better resource allocation: compare department work load 
Support staffing and resource planning .
Improved patient experience: analyze feedback tends
